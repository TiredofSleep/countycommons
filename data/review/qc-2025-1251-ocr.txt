Tucker called the meeting to order at 10:05 am.

Arnold and Cole.

decided to table this to look over all County financial status
Preliminary budgets were handed out to members present. Please see attached for
funds.
Vance presented her want for certificate pay. Judge mentioned that we need to add
certificate pay definitions to the Personnel Policy. After discussion, the committee
decided to table this to look over all County financial status.
Cole presented a clean up ordinance and explained every item. Johns made a motion to
pass it to full Quorum Court. Baumgardner seconded the motion. Motion carried 3-0.
With there being no further discussion Johns made a motion to adjourn the meeting.
Baumgardner seconded the motion.
Meeting adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
