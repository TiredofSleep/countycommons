REGULAR SESSION
FEBRUARY 9, 2026
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District
Courtroom. Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by Chief Deputy Clerk Crissi
Clark. The following justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne
Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe, Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn
Terry and Stuart Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Reverend Shepherd gave the
invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Baumgardner made the motion to adopt the agenda.
Justice Scott seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice
vote 10-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the minutes as
presented. Justice Hannah seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion
was carried by voice vote 10-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
Personnel Committee did not meet.
Budget Committee did not meet.
UNFINISHED IN : None
NEW BUSINESS:
1. Judge Tucker read the “Farm Bureau Week Proclamation”. There were
representatives from Farm Bureau standing as it was read. It was declared.
2. Justice Angle made a motion to suspend the rules to give Amy Simpson with
Extension Service more time to speak. Justice Angle seconded the motion. Motion
carried by voice vote 10-0. Amy presented her report with handouts and video
presentations. Justice Ankton commended the work that the students had achieved
and he witnessed.
Sponsor: Justice Angle

3. Justice Baumgardner made a motion to allow Judge Tucker to read the FBLA
declaration, Justice Scott seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 10-0.
Judge Tucker read the FBLA declaration and the week was declared. A
representative discussed going to State and National and students winning. It was
declared as FBLA week.

4. Shelley Short presented her fourth quarter EDCCC report. She gave quorum court a
handout and discussed while presenting a slide show presentation. Justice Hannah
asked if there was progress or tentative dates on the Caddo River Bridge. Shelly
Short answered by not knowing the exact date but estimating 2027. Justice Ankton
asked if the Mega Site was attracting any new business. Shelly Short explained that
there were several companies interested but none have committed.

RESOLUTION ALLOWING THE CLARK COUNTY JUDGE TO FINANCE 2026 MACK
TRUCK TERRA PRO 40YD FRONT LOAD SANITATION TRUCK.” Sponsor: Justice
Bledsoe. Justice Hannah seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice 10-0. Chief
Deputy Clark read the resolution in its entirety. -
Judge Baumgardner made a motion to adopt the resolution, Justice Thomas
seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice 10-0.

JUDGES REPORT:
Judge Tucker read about the disposal of property.

Artoched
Judge Tucker asked Quorum Court to pay the road and sanitation worker's overtime
while working during the ice storm. Justice Bledsoe made a motion to pay them
overtime. Justice Hannah seconded the motion. The motion was carried by voice
10-0.
The Road and Sanitation Department employees follow the same regular pay
schedule and holiday/closure policy as Courthouse employees. The County Judge
declared the Courthouse closed on January 26, 2026, through January 29, 2026.
Additionally, January 30, 2026, was designated as a six (6) hour workday.
During this closure period, Road and Sanitation employees were required to
continue working to maintain essential county services. The Quorum Court voted
and approved payment of overtime compensation for these employees.
Road and Sanitation employees received their regular straight-time pay for the
hours and days the Courthouse was officially closed, consistent with Courthouse —_
employee compensation. In addition, they were paid overtime compensation for

every hour actually worked on January 26-29, 2026, and for all hours worked beyond
— the six (6) hour workday on January 30, 2026. They were also paid overtime for any
additional hours worked over their standard work schedule during that pay period.

Judge Tucker introduced Cassidy West as the new assistant in the Judge’s office taking

Sandra’s place.

Other Business:

Judge Tucker spoke again about the Caddo River bridge, noting that there will be a walkway

for the community and stating that the projected date is 2027.

He reminded everyone about the Gurdon Chamber of Commerce having dinner at 6:30

tomorrow night.

Judge Tucker let the court know that we had several bids for new windows of the

courthouse. The grant was only $200,000 while the bids were well over that amount.

The courthouse clock is still in Florida where it was repaired due to the delicacy of

transportation and the dangerous icy road conditions. We do expect to get it back soon.

Judge Tucker let the court know that we are expecting FEMA money around the amount of
— $750,000 from the flood of April 2025.

Judge Tucker commended the road and sanitation crew for the hard work during ice. He

said that some phone calls were related to trash pickup, and he explained the process of

the pickup.

Justice Ankton asked if the contract for the ambulance was signed. Judge Tucker stated

that they had a meeting that morning and we're making progress.

With there being no further business, Justice Johns made a motion to adjourn. Justice

Thomas seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice 10-0.

Meeting adjourned at 7:15.
