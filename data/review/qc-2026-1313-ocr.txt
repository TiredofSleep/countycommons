The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom. Judge
Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by Chief Deputy Clerk Crissi Clark. The following justices
were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe, Tracy Drake,
Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jimmy King, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry and Stuart Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Terry gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda. Justice Angle
seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Scott made the motion to adopt the minutes as presented
and attach the Court Orders reflecting the Disposal of Property. Justice Angle seconded the motion.
With no further discussion, the motion was carried by voice vote 11-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
Personnel Committee did not meet.
ww Budget Committee met to discuss the Appropriation Ordinances on tonight’s agenda.
UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None
NEW BUSINESS:
1. Prosecutor Dan Turner gave his annual Hot Check Report.
2. Justice Baumgardner made a motion to adopt Proposed Resolution 2026-03 “A
RESOLUTION ALLOWING THE CLARK COUNTY JUDGE TO FINANCE (2) 2026 MACK
SANITATION TRUCKS”. Sponsor Justice Ankton. Justice Thomas seconded the motion.
Motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
3. Clint Mathis spoke on the Valor Ambulance Contract as Valor’s legal counsel.
Todd Turner spoke as the County’s legal counsel. There was conversation among all of the
Justices.
Justice Johns made a motion to table the issue of the contract and allow both legal
counsels and the judge to continue to work on the contract. Justice Hannah seconded the
motion. Motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake,
Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.
