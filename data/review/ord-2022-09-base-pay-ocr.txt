IN THE QUORUM COURT OF CLARK COUNTY, ARKANSAS
ORDINANCE NO. 2022- 04
AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING A BASE-PAY THRESHOLD FOR ALL
NEW FULL TIME COUNTY EMPLOYEES BEGINNING JANUARY 1, 2023
The Quorum Court of County of Clark, Arkansas hereby amends Ordinance
2021-27 and sets forth the following starting salary amounts for all new full time county
employees hired after January 1, 2023, per Attachment A;
The Quorum Court of County of Clark, Arkansas shall fix the annual salary for all
officials each year as required by A.C.A. 14-14-1204;
The Quorum Court of County of Clark, Arkansas may periodically review and
alter these established base pay wages;
Any part of this ordinance may be altered and amended from time to time as
necessary, or as requested, subject to approval of the full Quorum Court;
This Ordinance does not change any prior protocol or procedure for an individual
or department request for a subsequent pay raise; and
All provisions contained in the Clark County Personnel Policy shall remain in
effect.
Approved by the Clark County Quorum Court on this 12 day of December, 2022.
