CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT
REGULAR SESSION
December 9, 2024
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present. Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe,
Tracy Drake, David Forthman, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jimmy King, Albert Neal, and Jenna
Scott.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Neal gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda. Justice
Drake seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Baumgardner made the motion to adopt the regular
monthly minutes from the November 2024 meeting as presented. Justice Scott seconded the
motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
Justice Scott made a motion to adopt the special meeting minutes as presented. Justice Angle
seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
ND COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
Personnel Committee did not meet.
Budget Committee did not meet.
PUBLIC COMMENT: Joe Jackson spoke on the 911 consolidation.
UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None

2. Justice Johns made a motion to read Proposed Ordinance 2024-17 “AN ORDINANCE ~~
ADOPTING A BASE PAY THRESHOLD FOR ALL NEW FULL TIME COUNTY
EMPLOYEES BEGINNING JANUARY 1, 2025.” by title only. Sponsor: Justice King.
Justice Angle seconded the motion. Justice Scott voiced her concern regarding the jailer
raises only being $1k and everyone else’s being $2k. With no further discussion roll call
was as follows: Yea; Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Forthman, Hannah,
Johns, King, Neal. Nay; Scott. Motion carried 10-1.

Pruitt read the Proposed Ordinance by title only.

Justice Johns made a motion to adopt Ordinance 2024-17. Justice Baumgardner
seconded the motion. Justice Scott again voiced her objection. With no further
discussion roll call was as follows: Yea; Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake,
Forthman, Hannah, Johns, King, Neal. Nay; Scott. Motion carried 10-1.

3. Justice Bledsoe made a motion to read Proposed Ordinance 2024-18 “AN ORDINANCE
TO ADOPT YEAR 2025 BUDGET.” by title only. Sponsor: Justice Johns. Justice
Baumgardner seconded the motion. Justice Scott voiced her objection again. With no
further discussion roll call was as follows: Yea; Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe,
Drake, Forthman, Hannah, Johns, King, Neal. Nay; Scott. Motion carried 10-1.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Johns made a motion to adopt Ordinance 2024-18. Justice Hannah seconded

the motion. Justice Scott still objected due to the jailer pay. Justice Ankton voiced his N—
objection due to the livestream budget not being included. Justice Johns and Justice

Forthman made note that they discussed in the last budget committee meeting that the

livestream committee needs to check with the one of the school’s East programs

handling this to save the county time and money. With no further discussion roll call was

as follows: Yea; Angle, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Forthman, Hannah, Johns, King,

Neal. Nay; Ankton, Scott. Motion carried 9-2.

4. Justice Bledsoe made a motion to read A Proposed Ordinance 2024-19 “AN
ORDINANCE TO OBLIGATE THE REMAINING ARPA FUNDS ON THE BOOKS OF
THE COUNTY; AND TO DECLARE AN EMERGENCY.” By title only. Sponsor: Justice
Angle. Justice Angle seconded the motion. With no further discussion roll call was as
follows: Yea; Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Forthman, Hannah, Johns,
King, Neal, Scott. Motion carried 11-0.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.
Justice Angle made a motion to adopt Ordinance 2024-19. Justice Bledsoe seconded
the motion. With no further discussion roll call was as follows: Yea; Angle, Ankton,
Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Forthman, Hannah, Johns, King, Neal, Scott. Motion
carried 11-0.
