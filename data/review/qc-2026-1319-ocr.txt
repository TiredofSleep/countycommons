Summary of Appropriation Ordinance 2026-05
This ordinance includes several "cleanup" measures and necessary reimbursements
across various county departments to ensure fiscal accuracy:

o Administrative Adjustments: Finalizing transfers and "cleanups" for the Circuit
Clerk, Treasurer, and Prosecuting Attorney (including a duplicate claim
reimbursement).

« Grant & Insurance Reimbursements: Crediting funds back to the Sheriff (from

~~ Grant County) and Emergency 911 ($8,819.00 for insurance-covered hail damage).

« Critical Positions & Equipment: Securing $24,280.00 for the grant-funded Peer
Recovery position and funding essential equipment for the Road Fund (asphalt

Circuit Judge.

« Claims Coverage: Appropriating existing treasury balances for the Self-Insurance

Landfill Infrastructure Briefing
Judge Tucker provided a critical briefing regarding the county landfill. Two primary issues
require the Court's future attention:

1. Water Quality Remediation: Runoff pond solids are creating issues with
mandatory testing in Little Rock, necessitating costly remediation.

2. Capacity Planning: The current dumping cell has an estimated life span of
approximately 1.5 years. While new cells are licensed, they must be prepared for
use immediately.

The Judge noted that the $500,000.00 emergency bond may be required to address
these upcoming capital needs.
We respectfully request that these items be placed on the June 8th agenda for full
consideration.
Johns made a motion to adjourn and Bledsoe seconded the motion. Meeting adjourned at 11:32.
