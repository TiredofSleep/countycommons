MINUTES
CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT
REGULAR SESSION
February 10, 2025
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe,
Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jimmy King, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry, and Stuart
Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Scott gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda. Justice
Scott seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Scott made the motion to adopt the regular monthly
minutes from the January 13, 2025 meeting as presented. Justice Baumgardner seconded the
motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
Personnel Committee did not meet.
\—
Budget Committee did not meet.
PUBLIC COMMENTS: Doug Nelson gave a presentation on a proposed Summer Youth
Program through Men United.
Justice Ankton made a motion to take the request for $30,000 to the budget committee. Justice
Thomas seconded the motion. With no further discussion roll call was as follows: Yea; Angle,
Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry, Thomas. Motion
carried 11-0.
Pruitt will inform Mr. Nelson of the next Budget Committee Meeting.
