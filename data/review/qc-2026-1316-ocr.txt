CONTRACT FOR AMBULANCE

SERVICES IN CLARK COUNTY

AND THE RESPECTIVE CITIES

This Agreement made and entered into effective asofthe __ day of
s , by and between Clark County, Arkansas, (the County), and the

Respective Cities of Arkadelphia, Gurdon, Caddo Valley, and Amity Arkansas (the
CITIES), hereinafter referred to as the GOVERNING BODIES and Pafford Medical
Services, Inc. (hereinafter referred to as "Pafford") now WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the GOVERNING BODIES, in the exercise of its authority to protect the
health, safety and welfare of the inhabitants of Clark County, Arkansas considers the
providing of affordable and reliable advanced life support and ambulance service to be
within the scope of its authority; and

WHEREAS, Pafford is an ambulance service incorporated under the laws of the State of
Arkansas and licensed by the Arkansas Department of Health; and, -

WHEREAS, the County issued Requests for Qualifications (RFQs) for
emergency Medical Services on July 18, 2025; and

WHEREAS, Pafford submitted responses to the RFQs; and

WHEREAS, the GOVERNING BODIES and Pafford wish to enter into a binding
agreement for ambulance service in Clark County, Arkansas.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the covenants and other
considerations and agreements stated herein, the parties agree that the RFQs are
hereby incorporated by reference and further agree as follows:

1. GRANT OF FRANCHISE. The GOVERNING BODIES hereby grant to Pafford the
franchise and exclusive privilege of operating and maintaining an Advanced Life
Support ambulance service in and through, and over all roads, streets, avenues, alleys,
sidewalks, and public grounds of Clark County, and Pafford is hereby granted the right
to ingress and egress thereon for the purpose of aforesaid during the term of this
franchise. This franchise will begin at 12:00 a.m. on May 1, 2026, and will end at 11:59
p.m. on April 30, 2029. Agreement shall be for an initial term of three (3) years. Upon
expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for one (1)
additional term of (3) years upon mutual agreement of the parties. If either Party does not wish to

extend the Agreement, written notice shall be given to the other Party not less than ninety (90)
days prior to the expiration of the then-existing term, subject to annual appropriation by the
GOVERNING BODIES.
2. EXCLUSIVE NATURE OF AGREEMENT. Pursuant to the grant of
authority pursuant to Ark. Code Ann. § 14-266-105, the GOVERNING
BODIES agree that this Agreement is exclusive to Pafford and the GOVERNING
BODIES will not contract these services with any other contractors for the duration of the
period of this Agreement.
3. SERVICES: Pafford agrees to provide emergency medical services to all residents
of Clark County, adhering to and meeting the criteria listed herein.

4. MUTUAL AID: Pafford will make every effort to maintain backfill (mutual aid)
agreements with the surrounding counties’ ambulance services as required by
Arkansas EMS Rules as governed by the Arkansas Department of Health. Such
agreements shall help provide support during times of peak demand or disaster.
Likewise, Pafford may make its local resources available in reciprocity to backfill
partners, if available.

5. STAFFING: Pafford agrees to provide ambulance services with a minimum of one (1)
paramedic ambulance staffed twenty-four (24) hours a day based in Gurdon, AR as well as two
(2) paramedic ambulances staffed twenty-four (24) hours a day based in Arkadelphia, AR.

6. RESPONSE TIME STANDARDS: Pafford understands that the County and Respective
Cities have set forth a requirement for all emergency responses to have an ALS equipped
ambulance on-scene within the designated response time not less than 90% of the time.
This will be measured and tracked.

Below is a list of projected, reasonable, and safely obtainable response times to each
respective City. These times are listed from shortest response resource location first,
followed by the next closest response location within the county, in the event that the
closest resource is already committed to a response at the time of the request.
Response time compliance shall be measured as the time from the time the ambulance received
the call for service until an ambulance arrives on scene. In addition to the reporting
requirements set forth herein, Pafford shall comply with all reporting, documentation, and
compliance requirements specified in the RFQ, which are incorporated by reference as if fully
set forth.

Response time for purposes of this Agreement shall be measured as total elapsed time
from dispatch notification to on-scene arrival, and shall include the reaction time interval
recognized under ADH EMS Rules, Definition 46 (effective October 28, 2024), which is
defined as the period from when the call is received by dispatch until the unit is enroute.
The response times listed above represent total on-scene arrival times inclusive of that
interval. Travel time alone shall not be used as the sole measure of compliance.
Response time standards shall not apply during periods of inclement weather, natural
disasters, unusually high call demand as defined as call volume exceeding 2.5 standard
deviations about the call volume for that hour of day and day of week for the previous 20
weeks, if the first or second ambulance, as referenced above is not located in the EMS
station at the time of call, hazardous road conditions, mass casualty incidents, or other
circumstances beyond the reasonable control of Pafford that may affect the safety of
personnel or the public.

7. EQUIPMENT AND VEHICLES: Pafford agrees to maintain the ambulance vehicles in
good working condition and to provide all necessary and needed supplies for the ambulance
vehicles operations. Backup vehicles will be supplied for the occasion of a frontline vehicle

21. DEFAULT. IfPafford fails to perform the services which it is obligated to perform
hereunder or shall violate any material provision which jeopardizes the health, safety, or
welfare of the residents in this service area of this agreement, Pafford shall be in default. If
Pafford's financial condition, managerial situation, operating condition, staffing situation or
equipment or supplies condition shall become such that it poses a threat to the health and
safety of the inhabitants of the service area, Pafford shall be deemed in default. Determination
of default will be the responsibility of the Clark County Judge and the Mayors of the
Respective Cities. Pafford will be notified in writing if a default exists and will be given ten
(10) working days to correct. Failure
to correct the default will be considered a breach of contract, subject to early termination of
the contract.

Defaults, performance deficiencies, and corrective actions shall be determined using the

the RFQ, which are incorporated herein by reference.
