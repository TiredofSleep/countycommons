BUDGET COMMITTEE MEETING By SANIT ROLLA D.C.
October 8, 2025

The meeting was called to order by Chairman BJ Johns at 10:06 am.

MEMBERS PRESENT: Wayne Baumgardner, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns& Lewellyn Terry.

OTHERS PRESENT: Raymond Funderburk, Fred Phillips, Brian Daniell, Rhonda Cole, Jason

Watson, Dan Turner, Karen Spradlin, Karen Arnold, Mona Vance & Tracy Pruitt.

Please see the attached spreadsheet for Funds that were discussed.

In the discussion, Karen Arnold gave her projections for next year.

The Justices asked Rhonda Cole to figure in a $2k employee increase across the board and bring
those numbers to them at the next meeting.

Mona Vance asked the board to consider giving her deputies certificate pay. They advised her to
take it to the Personnel Committee.

- Justice Johns wanted an update on our insurance problem. Tracy Pruitt stated that the Insurance i

contracts can only be signed by the Judge but, because he was absent, she could
give the information that she had been given from asking Hatcher Agency
directly. Colt had informed her that we do have insurance, but it is not completed
in United Healthcare's system, therefore employees will need to call him directly
so he can explain the situation and hopefully it will be fixed this week. She had
passed that on to the Elected Officials and was still getting phone calls from
employees who were being asked to pay cash or reschedule their appointments.
The Justices who were present stated that they were getting phone calls. Greg
Hatcher and Colt Fason’s phone numbers were given out to the Justices.

With there being no further discussion, Justice Johns made a motion to adjourn. Justice

Baumgardner seconded the motion.

The meeting Adjourned at 11:47 am.
