CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT Sauna Wedidh..
REGULAR SESSION
AUGUST 11, 2025
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe,
Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, B.J. Johns, Jimmy King, Llewellyn Terry, and Stuart Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Terry gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda after
changing the date from 8/1 to 8/11. Justice Hannah seconded the motion. With no further
discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 10-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Hannah made the motion to adopt the minutes from
the July 2025. Justice Thomas seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion
carried by voice vote 10-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
Personnel Committee did not meet.
Budget Committee did not meet.
UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None
NEW BUSINESS:

1. Betsy Fisher gave her annual Library report.

2. Justice Angle had a Summary Sheet for Cody Wright to speak on Advocating for Dash
Cam/Body Cams and he did not show up.

3. Justice Johns made a motion to read A Proposed Appropriation Ordinance 2025-15 “AN
APPROPRIATION ORDINANCE TO AMEND THE 2025 CLARK COUNTY ANNUAL
BUDGET ORDIANCE NO. 2024-18 AND AMENDMENTS THERETO; TO
APPROPRIATE PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT RECOGNITION PAYMENTS; AND
FOR OTHER PURPOSES.” By title only. Sponsor: Justice Bledsoe. Justice King
seconded the motion. With no further discussion roll call was as follows: Yea; Angle,
Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Terry, and Thomas. Motion
carried 10-0.

Pruitt read the Resolution by title only.
