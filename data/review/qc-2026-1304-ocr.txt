— CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT
REGULAR SESSION
JANUARY 12, 2026
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District
Courtroom. Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt.
The following justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner,
Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry and Stuart Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Terry gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Ankton made the motion to adopt the agenda. Justice
Hannah seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote
9-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Thomas made the motion to adopt the minutes as
presented. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the
motion carried by voice vote 9-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
= Personnel Committee did not meet.
Budget Committee met. Justice Johns gave his update when we discussed the
Appropriation Ordinance.
UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None
NEW BUSINESS:
1. Justice Terry made a motion to read A Proposed Ordinance 2025-07 by title only for its
third and final reading. “AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING THE PUBLICATION OF COUNTY JOB
OPPORTUNITIES AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.” Sponsor: Justice Baumgardner. Justice
Angle seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 9-0.
Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.
Justice Scott made a motion to adopt the Proposed Ordinance. Justice Angle seconded the
motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton,
Baumgardner, Drake, Hannah, Johns, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

2. JD Windham gave his Quarterly Ambulance report. Announcing they are moving into the -
Old Aaron’s building located at 1520 Pine St. He wants to go by fire districts for response
times instead of zip codes. They have yearly memberships offered to the community now.
3. Justice Scott made a motion to read A Proposed Ordinance 2026-01 “AN
ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE EXCHANGE OF PROPERTY BETWEEN COUNTIES.” By title
only. Sponsor: Justice Baumgardner. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. Motion
carried by voice vote 9-0.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Scott made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Angle seconded the motion.
With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner,
Drake, Hannah, Johns, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

4. Justice Terry made a motion to read A Proposed Resolution 2026-01 “A RESOLUTION
ALLOWING THE CLARK COUNTY JUDGE TO FINANCE A 2025 CATERPILLAR GRADER WITH
SOUTHERN BANCORP”. Sponsor: Justice Baumgardner. By title only. Justice Baumgardner
seconded the motion. With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote 9-0.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Angle made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Drake seconded the motion.
Motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Drake, Hannah, Johns,
Scott, Terry and Thomas.

5. Justice Baumgardner made a motion to read A Proposed Appropriation Ordinance
2026-02 “AN ORDIANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO 2025-21 APPROPRIATING FUNDS;
MAKING SPECIFIC ADDITIONS/DELETIONS TO THE CLARK COUNTY BUDGET FOR 2026.”
Sponsor: Justice Johns. By title only. Justice Thomas seconded the motion. With no further
discussion, motion carried by voice vote11-0.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Ankton made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Scott seconded the motion.
With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner,
Drake, Hannah, Johns, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

Justice Terry and Justice Ankton announced the following activities for Martin Luther King,
Jr. Day & Black History Month:
1/18/2026 @ 4pm ~ Celebration of the life and legacy of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. A special
program featuring worship, reflection, and inspiration with special guest speaker
Honorable Justice Michael Ankton at St. Paul A.M.E. Church.
1/19/2026 @ 11:30am ~ Chili Supper honoring City of Arkadelphia Sanitation Workers at
Mt. Olive Baptist Church with speaker Zeke Allen. All are invited but Sanitation workers will
eat first.
1/19/2026 @ 2pm ~ Martin Luther King Jr. Parade from Mt. Olive Baptist Church to the
Courthouse Square Gazebo.
1/19/2026 @ 3:30pm ~ 2026 Clark County Candidate Forum at the National Guard Armory.
All Candidates will have an opportunity to speak.
2/22/2026 @ 3pm ~ The Clark County NAACP proudly presents the 5th Annual Black

~ History Celebration as we reflect on “A Century of Black History Commemorations.” At St.
Paul A.M.E. Church. This year’s celebration will feature a powerful panel of Black history
collaborationists: Hon. Holand L. Gosey, Vice Mayor of Arkadelphia, Mrs. Ashley Wesley,
Black History Month Committee Sponsor, Arkadelphia High School, and Hon. Rev.
Licwellyn E Terry Sr, Pastor of Mt. Olive MB Church & Clark County Justice of the Peace.
Guests are encouraged to wear African attire or Red, Black, or Green in honor of our
heritage. Refreshments will be served following the program.
With there being no further business, Justice Johns made a motion to adjourn at 6:06pm.
Justice Baumgaftdner seconded the motion. Motion passed by voice vote 9-0.
