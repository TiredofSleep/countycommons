— CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COUR}, D.C.
REGULAR SESSION
NOVEMBER 10, 2025

The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe,
Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry and Stuart Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Terry gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda. Justice
Hannah seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 10-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Hannah made the motion to adopt the minutes as
presented. Justice Scott seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by
voice vote 10-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:

Personnel Committee did not meet.

Budget Committee met. Justice Johns reported that they have started reviewing the

2026 budget.
UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None
NEW BUSINESS:

1. Justice Drake and Justice Ankton wanted it added to the minutes for today’s meeting
that the 2 new Sheriff's deputy positions be reevaluated every year so that when the
Collector's Automation Fund can no longer fund Personal Services these positions
shall not be paid from County General as originally agreed upon in Personnel and
Budget Committees.

2. Justice Johns made a motion to add the following verbiage after Section 3
“(2) newspaper of general circulation,” Justice Scott seconded the motion.
Motion carried by voice vote 10-0.

3. Justice Scott made a motion to read A Proposed Ordinance 2025-07 with the
above mentioned addition for its first reading. “AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING
THE PUBLICATION OF COUNTY JOB OPPORTUNITIES AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES.” Sponsor: Justice Baumgardner.

Justice Ankton seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried
by voice vote 11-0.

DISPOSAL REPORT:
JUDGE TUCKERS REPORT: IR
Judge brought up to the Court that a decision had been made last year to give
employees a one time salary adjustment of $1500 with the following limitations: All full time
employees whom have been employed full time from 1/1/25-12/31/25 will receive the full $1500,
full time employees whom have been employed over 6 months by 12/31/2025 will receive $750,
full time employees whom have been employed for less than 6 months but are on the county
insurance by 12/31/2025 will receive $375 and part time employees who have worked at least
1040 hours by 12/31/2025 will receive $750.

2026 Budgets were handed out to every Justice, they were asked to read through the
entire thing and ask any questions before the December meeting.
Tomorrow is Veterans day and all County offices will be closed.
The Veteran Services will have a parade at 10 am at the comer of the old Piggly Wiggly.
HSU will have an event from noon-2 at the Garrison Center.
Judge asked the Court for their blessing on allowing the Courthouse to close on 12/25 and
12/26 instead of 12/24 & 12/25. They were all ok with this.
All of the Justices thanked Rhonda Cole for her hard work on the Budget. Justice Bledsoe
added thanks to Justice Johns and Judge added thanks to Clerk Pruitt.
The Valor contract update was mentioned and Judge informed them that it is the final review
stages.
The 4H building was brought up and Judge informed that there is no new news.
