ORDINANCE NUMBER 2025-02
AN ORDINANCE AMMENDING AND ADOPTING A BASE PAY THRESHOLD FOR ALL
NEW FULL TIME COUNTY EMPLOYEES BEGINNING JANUARY 1, 2025
The Quorum Court of County of Clark, Arkansas hereby amends Ordinance Number
2024-17 and sets forth the following starting salary amounts for all new full-time employees
hired after January 1, 2025, per Attachment A;
The Quorum Court of County of Clark, Arkansas shall fix the annual salary for all
officials each year as required A.C.A. 14-14-1204;
The Quorum Court of County of Clark. Arkansas may periodically review and alter these
established base pay wages;
Any part of this Ordinance may be altered and amended from time to time as necessary,
or as requested, subject to approval of the full Quorum Court;
This Ordinance does not change any prior protocol or procedure for an individual or
department request for a subsequent pay raise; and
— All provisions contained in the Clark County Personnel Policy shall remain in effect.
