REGULAR SESSION
January 13, 2025
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe,
Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jimmy King, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry, and Stuart
Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Terry gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda. Justice
Baumgardner seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote
11-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Scott made the motion to adopt the regular monthly
minutes from the December 9, 2024 meeting as presented. Justice Angle seconded the motion.
With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
Justice Scott made a motion to adopt the minutes from the January 2, 2025 special meeting as
presented. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion
carried by voice vote 11-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
Personnel Committee did not meet.
Budget Committee did not meet.
UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None
NEW BUSINESS:
