The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Tracy Drake, BJ
Johns, Jimmy King & Jenna Scott. Stuart Thomas and Vanilla Hannah arrived before New
Business #2.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Dr. Shepherd gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda. Justice
Angle seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 8-0.

as presented. Justice Angle seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried
by voice vote 8-0.
