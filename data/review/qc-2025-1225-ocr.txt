\ CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT
REGULAR SESSION
MAY 12, 2025
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton (after agenda and minutes were adopted),
Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe, Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jimmy King,
Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry, and Stuart Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Terry gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda as
presented. Justice Angle seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by
voice vote 10-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Johns made the motion to adopt the minutes from
the April 2025 meeting as presented. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. With no
further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 10-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
Personnel Committee did not meet.
\— Budget Committee did not meet.
UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None
NEW BUSINESS:

1. Doug Nelson spoke on the Men United Youth Program. He was asked to bring back
additional information and meet with the Budget Committee.

2. Judge Tucker presented the Arkansas Foster Care Month Proclamation.

3. Justice Scott made a motion to allow Shelley Short more than 5 minutes to give her
EDCCC report. Justice King seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion
carried 11-0.

Short gave her report.
