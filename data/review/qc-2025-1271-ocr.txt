CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT gy, LLL RATA
BUDGET COMMITTEE MEETING
DECEMBER 4, 2025
MEMBERS PRESENT: Wayne Baumgardner, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Lewellyn Terry
OTHERS PRESENT: Troy Tucker, Ron Addington, Rhonda Cole, Tracy Pruitt, Karen Arnold,
Brian Daniell, Tate Chanler, Raymond Funderburk, Penny Ross.
Tucker and Cole explained the Clean Up Ordinance. Justice Johns made a motion to approve
Ordinance 2025-24 to move to full Quorum Court. Justice Hannah seconded the motion. Motion
carried by voice vote 4-0.
Ross addressed the Committee on the Peer Specialist pay. The position was agreed on with a
maximum pay of $49,875.00. With the insurance rates increasing this will put her over by
$3113.72. Ross informed the Committee that she spoke with the director of the Grant, and he
informed her that this is a problem across the state so the 2026 grant should be increased to
$55,000.00. Johns made a motion to temporarily allow the $3113.72 to come from County
General only until the grant comes in. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. The motion
carried by voice vote 4-0.
~ Chanler made a proposal to allow his position to receive Certificate Pay. At the moment, he has
4 which would total $3000.00 (see attachment). Justice Terry made a motion to send this to the
Personnel Committee and if approved include in a January clean-up ordinance. Justice Hannah
seconded the motion. The motion carried by voice vote 4-0.
Due to no set rules on Certificate Pay/Further Education for the County as a whole, Judge Tucker
suggested that there be set rules for the County as a whole and not just accept each office a
different set of rules. Justice Johns made a motion to ask The Personnel Committee to make said
set rules for the entire County instead of different rules for every department. Justice
Baumgardner seconded the motion. Motion carried 4-0.
Arnold discussed adding an Appropriation Ordinance to this month’s QC meeting to establish a
special revenue fund (grants) to be called the Peer Recovery Specialist Grant Fund to keep up
with all monies dealing with this Grant Position. This will be placed on a cleanup ordinance in
January.
With there being no further discussion, Justice Johns made a motion to adjourn. Justice
Baumgardner seconded the motion.
