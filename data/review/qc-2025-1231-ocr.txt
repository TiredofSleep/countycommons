CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT
REGULAR SESSION
JUNE 9, 2025
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present: Andrea Angle, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe, Tracy Drake, Vanilla
Hannah, Jimmy King, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry, and Stuart Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Scott gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Baumgardner made the motion to adopt the agenda as
presented. Justice Bledsoe seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried
by voice vote 9-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Hannah made the motion to adopt the minutes from
the May 2025 meeting as presented. Justice Scott seconded the motion. With nofurther
discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 9-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
Personnel Committee did not meet.
Budget Committee did not meet.

UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None
NEW BUSINESS:

1. Sheriff Watson gave an update on the jail. He made a recommendation to the court to
appoint Justice Scott to be on the PONI Board. Sheriff Watson advised the Court that he,
Raymond Funderburk, Melanie Morris, Troy Tucker and now Justice Scott are on the
board. Justice Baumgardner made the motion and Justice Bledsoe seconded the
motion. With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote 9-0.

2. Justice Baumgardner made a motion to read A Proposed Ordinance 2025-14 “AN
ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO 2025-07 APPROPRIATING
FUNDS; MAKING SPECIFIC ADDITIONS/DELETIONS TO THE CLARK
COUNTY BUDGET FOR 2025.” By title only. Sponsor: Justice Baumgardner.
Justice Scott seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by
voice vote 9-0.
