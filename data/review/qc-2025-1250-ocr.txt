CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT 4
PERSONNEL COMMITTEE MEETING -C.
October 27, 2025
MEMBERS PRESENT: Jimmy King, Michael Ankton, Tracy Drake, Stuart Thomas.
OTHERS PRESENT: Raymond Funderburk, Rhonda Cole, Jason Watson, Troy Tucker, Mona
Vance, Crissi Clark & Tracy Pruitt.
Proposed Ordinance 2025-07 was presented due to it being pulled at the March
10, 2025 regular Quorum Court meeting by Justice Bledsoe with Justice
Baumgardner seconding it to send it to the Personnel Committee. The Committee
could not see a reason that it needed to be presented to them so Justice King made
a motion to send it back to full Quorum Court. Justice Thomas seconded the
motion. Motion carried by voice vote 4-0.
After some discussion of the budget falling short as presented prior to adding
them, Sheriff Watson proposed his need for two new full time deputy positions.
Justice Ankton made a motion to tentatively approve the positions to send to The
Budget Committee. Justice Thomas seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice
vote 4-0.
Assessor Vance presented adding Certificate Pay to herself and her deputies who
receive and/or have already received Certificates from training. Due to the budget
falling short, Justice King made a motion to tentatively approve her request for
the Assessor and her staff to all be eligible for Certificate Pay if the funding is
available in the budget. Justice Drake seconded the motion. Motion carried by
voice vote 4-0.
Per Justice Scott’s request, a new Al policy was discussed. Justice King made a
motion to have our legal counsel look at the proposal. Justice Thomas seconded
the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 4-0.
The Public Defender had sent a request for 1.5 new positions. Due to no
representation from their office being present their request was tabled until the
next meeting.
With there being no further discussion, Justice Thomas made a motion to adjourn. Justice
Ankton seconded the motion.
The meetirg adjourned at 4:42 pm.
