MEMORANDUM

Tx: Clark County Justices of the Peace

FROM: Todd Turner

Date: April 13, 2026

RE: Update on Clark County v. AT&T,

Arkansas Court of Appeals No. CV-24-54

I wanted to update you on the lawsuit that Clark County initiated against
AT&T. That case is now on its second appeal at the Court of Appeals.

The County sued AT&T on March 17, 2017 after making multiple requests
to get information about various charges which had appeared on the County’s
telephone bills. After it was unable to get information about phone charges from
AT&T, the County learned that many other Arkansas counties had been charged

_ for the same fees. Since the challenged practices affected multiple counties, Clark
County requested that the case be treated as a class action.

AT&T initially responded to the County’s complaint by arguing that an
arbitration provision in AT&T’s website “Terms and Conditions” prevented the
County from being able to pursue a remedy in court. That argument was rejected
by the trial court and was affirmed by the Arkansas Court of Appeals on March,
14, 2018 when it ruled that Clark County’s lawsuit against AT&T could proceed in
court.

The case was certified as a Class Action and a trial was held on December 9,
2022. The trial court took the case under advisement. Eleven months later, on
October 30, 2023, the trial court ruled against the counties and in favor of AT&T.

Clark County appealed the trial court’s ruling and the parties filed appeal
Briefs in June, 2024. The Arkansas Association of Counties also filed an amicus
curiae Brief in support of the counties.

The Court of Appeals has now announced that it will hold oral arguments on

ww the appeal on April 29, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. at the Arkansas Supreme Court. I will let
the Quorum Court know how the Court rules.
