MINUTES
CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT
REGULAR SESSION
JULY 14, 2025
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe,
Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, B.J. Johns, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry (came in late) , and
Stuart Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Scott gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda as
presented. Justice Hannah seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried
by voice vote 9-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Scott made the motion to adopt the minutes from the
June 2025 meeting with the correction from “RFP to RFQ” in the Judge’s notes. Justice Thomas
seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 9-0.
COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:
Personnel Committee did not meet.
Budget Committee did not meet.
UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None
NEW BUSINESS:

1. Justice Scott made a motion to read A Proposed Resolution 2025-05 “A
RESOLUTION TO RECOGNIZE THE ARKANSAS 250. COMMISSION AS IT
BEGINS ITS WORK TO CELEBRATE ARKANSAS'S RICH TWO-HUNDRED
FIFTY-YEAR HISTORY FROM THE SIGNING OF THE DECLARATION OF
INDEPENDENCE ON JULY 4, 1776, THROUGH JULY 4, 2026..” By title only.
Sponsor: Justice Ankton. Justice Hannah seconded the motion. With no further
discussion roll call was as follows: Yea; Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake,
Hannah, Johns, Scott, Terry, and Thomas. Motion carried 10-0.

Pruitt read the Resolution by title only.

Justice Johns made a motion to adopt the Resolution. Justice Ankton seconded the
motion. With no further discussion roll call was as follows: Yea; Angle, Ankton,
Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, Scott, Terry, and Thomas. Motion carried
10-0.

supposed to be looking at them July 21, 2025.

placing lights and guards at the Richwood Railroad Crossing.
RFQ’s will go out this Friday. They will only be excepted in person and must be sealed
