CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT
SPECIAL SESSION
January 2, 2025
The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 10:00 am in the District Courtroom.
Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following
justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe,
Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, Jimmy King, Jenna Scott, and Llewellyn Terry.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Dr. Shepherd gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Baumgardner made the motion to adopt the agenda
with the correction of the time being 10 am instead of 5:30 pm. Justice Bledsoe seconded the
motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 9-0.
NEW BUSINESS:
1. Judge Blake Batson performed the 2025 Justice of the Peace swearing in ceremony for
all justices present.
2. Justice Scott made a motion to read the Proposed Ordinance 2025-01 “AN
ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING THE TIME AND PLACE OF THE REGULAR MONTHLY
MEETING OF THE CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT 2025-2026.” Sponsor: Justice
N— Angle. Justice Hannah seconded the motion. With no further discussion the motion
carried by voice vote 9-0.
Pruitt read the Ordinance in its entirety.
Justice Baumgardner made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Angle seconded
the motion. With there being no further discussion, roll call was as follows: Yea; Angle,
Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, King, Scott, and Terry. Motion Carried
9-0.
