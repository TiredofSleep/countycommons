Budget Committee Meeting County Clerk, Clark County
11/05/2025 By SAMaodl Nowddn 5»
10:00 a.m.
Members Present: BJ Johns, Wayne Baumgardner, Terry Lewellyn & Vanilla Hannah
Others Present: Fred Phillips, Karen Arnold, Mona Vance, Tracy Pruitt, Rhonda Cole, Troy
Tucker, Jason Watson, Crissi Clark, Eric Gillis, Renee Pritchard, Clint Dewitt,
Raymond Funderburk and Brian Daniel.
Justice Johns called the meeting to order at 10:04 am.
Public Defender Clint Dewitt explained why he is wanting the new full time county position. Justice Johns
made a motion to table the decision until the end of the meeting. Justice Hannah seconded the motion.
Motion carried by voice vote 4-0.
Gillis explained his expenses to the Board as they had questions on possible cuts to the budget.
Pruitt asked for Diedra Middleton, The Election Coordinator to receive salary increases as the County's
Full Time Employees receive them. Justice Johns made a motion to approve that said Election
Coordinator, Diedra Middleton will receive salary increases as the County’s Full Time Employees receive
them. This will not carry over to any new Election Coordinator. Justice Hannah seconded the motion.
Motion carried by voice vote 4-0.
Please see attached for funds discussed today.
The following positions & additional pay were also approved to go to Full Quorum Court (notes are
attached for this as well including costs).
1000/105 The Assessor and three of her deputies to start receiving Certificate Pay, totaling $9600 for
2025 (see attached)
1000/400 The Sheriff's office adding 2 new full time deputies, totaling $129,100.
3026/417 The Public Defenders office to add 1 full time deputy position, totaling $50,590.61
The Library budget would not be available until after the Library Board met today so Justice Johns made
a motion to approve their budget as long as it does not exceed what it did last year. Justice Terry
seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 4-0. We did receive the budget and there were no
increases other than Personal Services.
Justice Johns made a motion to approve Appropriation Ordinance 2025-18 to go to full Quorum Court
Monday. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 4-0.
Pruitt and Arnold voiced their concerns regarding Grant Funded positions that no longer meet their
requirements so are costing County General extra monies. They both agreed to get the Board more
information on every Grant position along with its current pay.
With their being no further discussion, Justice Johns made a motion to adjourn the meeting. Justice
Baumgardner seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 4-0.
Meeting adjourned at 12:45 p.m.
