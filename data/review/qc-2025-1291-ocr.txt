2. Justice Scott made a motion to read A Proposed Appropriation Ordinance 2025-19 “AN
ORDINANCE TO LEVY TAXES ON REAL PROPERTY AND PERSONAL PROPERTY FOR THE
2025 COLLECTIBLE IN 2026.” Sponsor: Justice Ankton.

Justice Ankton seconded the motion. With no further discussion, motion carried by voice
vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry
and Thomas.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Ankton made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Drake seconded the
motion. Motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake,
Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

3. Justice Terry made a motion to read A Proposed Ordinance 2025-21 “AN
ORDINANCE TO ADOPT YEAR 2026 ANNUAL OPERATING BUDGET.” By title only. Sponsor:
Justice Johns.
Justice Hannah seconded the motion. With no further discussion, motion carried by voice
vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry
and Thomas.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.
Justice Johns made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Angle seconded the motion.
With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner,
Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.
4, Justice Scott made a motion to suspend the rules to read A Proposed Ordinance
2025-22 “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING AND ADOPTING A BASE PAY THRESHOLD FOR ALL
NEW FULL TIME COUNTY EMPLOYEES BEGINNING JANUARY 1, 2026”. Sponsor: Justice
Johns. By title only. Justice Angle seconded the motion. With no further discussion, motion
carried by voice vote 11-0.
Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.
Justice Baumgardner made a motion to suspend the rules for a second reading of the
Ordinance by title only. Justice Angle seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote
11-0.
Justice King made a motion to read the Ordinance by title only. Justice Baumgardner
seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.
