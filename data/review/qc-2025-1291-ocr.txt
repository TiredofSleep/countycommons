CLARK COUNTY QUORUM COURT
REGULAR SESSION
DECEMBER 8, 2025

The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District
Courtroom. Judge Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt.
The following justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner,
Zach Bledsoe, Tracy Drake, Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jimmy King, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn
Terry and Stuart Thomas.

County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Terry gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Scott made the motion to adopt the agenda after
removing # 3, Proposed Ordinance 2025-20 as Counsel advised it is not needed. Justice
Hannah seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote
11-0.

DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Scott made the motion to adopt the minutes as
presented. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the
motion carried by voice vote 11-0.

COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:

Personnel Committee did not meet.

Budget Committee met. Justice Johns gave updates throughout the meeting on the
Ordinances discussed.
UNFINISHED BUSINESS: None
NEW BUSINESS:

1. Justice Baumgardner made a motion to read A Proposed Ordinance 2025-07 by title
only for its second reading. “AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING THE PUBLICATION OF COUNTY
JOB OPPORTUNITIES AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.” Sponsor: Justice Baumgardner.
Justice King seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 11-0.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.
Justice Baumgardner made a motion to place the Proposed Ordinance on its third and final
reading in next month’s meeting. Justice Scott seconded the motion. With no further
discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 11-0.

2. Justice Scott made a motion to read A Proposed Appropriation Ordinance 2025-19 “AN
ORDINANCE TO LEVY TAXES ON REAL PROPERTY AND PERSONAL PROPERTY FOR THE
2025 COLLECTIBLE IN 2026.” Sponsor: Justice Ankton.

Justice Ankton seconded the motion. With no further discussion, motion carried by voice
vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry
and Thomas.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Ankton made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Drake seconded the
motion. Motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake,
Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

3. Justice Terry made a motion to read A Proposed Ordinance 2025-21 “AN
ORDINANCE TO ADOPT YEAR 2026 ANNUAL OPERATING BUDGET.” By title only. Sponsor:
Justice Johns.
Justice Hannah seconded the motion. With no further discussion, motion carried by voice
vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry
and Thomas.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.
Justice Johns made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Angle seconded the motion.
With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner,
Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.
4, Justice Scott made a motion to suspend the rules to read A Proposed Ordinance
2025-22 “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING AND ADOPTING A BASE PAY THRESHOLD FOR ALL
NEW FULL TIME COUNTY EMPLOYEES BEGINNING JANUARY 1, 2026”. Sponsor: Justice
Johns. By title only. Justice Angle seconded the motion. With no further discussion, motion
carried by voice vote 11-0.
Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.
Justice Baumgardner made a motion to suspend the rules for a second reading of the
Ordinance by title only. Justice Angle seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote
11-0.
Justice King made a motion to read the Ordinance by title only. Justice Baumgardner
seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 11-0.
Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Scott made a motion to Suspend the rules for a third and final reading of the
Ordinance AND to read the Ordinance by title only. Justice Angle seconded the motion.
With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner,
Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

Pruitt read the motion by title only.

Justice Scott made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Angle seconded the motion.
With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner,
Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

5. Justice Hannah made a motion to read A Proposed Appropriation Ordinance 2025-
23 “AN ORDIANCE TO ESTABLISH A SPECIAL REVENUE FUND (GRANTS) TO BE CALLED
THE PEER RECOVERY SPECIALIST GRANT FUND”. Sponsor: Justice Hannah. By title only.
Justice Bledsoe seconded the motion. With no further discussion, motion carried by voice
vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry
and Thomas.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Scott made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Hannah seconded the
motion. With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton,
Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

6. Justice Johns made a motion to read A Proposed Ordinance 2025-24 “AN
ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO 2025-18 APPROPRIATING FUNDS; MAKING
SPECIFIC ADDITIONS/DELETIONS TO THE CLARK COUNTY BUDGET FOR 2025” Sponsor:
Justice Johns. By title only. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. With no further
discussion, motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe,
Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Johns made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Scott seconded the motion.
With no further discussion, motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner,
Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns, King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.
