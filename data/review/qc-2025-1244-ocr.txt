The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom.

justices were present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Tracy Drake,
Vanilla Hannah, BJ Johns, Jimmy King, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry, and Stuart Thomas.
County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Dr. Shepherd gave the invocation.
APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Scott made the motion to adopt the agenda after adding
to allow 3 minutes for each person wishing to speak on the ambulance issue. Justice Hannah
seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 10-0.
DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice Johns made the motion to adopt the minutes from
the August 2025 meeting as presented. Justice Angle seconded the motion. With no further
discussion, the motion carried by voice vote 10-0.
