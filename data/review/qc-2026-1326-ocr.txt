CLARK COUNTY QUORUM coursy_ SAMI AU oe.
REGULAR SESSION
JUNE 9, 2026

The Clark County Quorum Court met on the above date at 5:30 pm in the District Courtroom. Judge

Troy Tucker presided. The roll was called by County Clerk Tracy Pruitt. The following justices were

present: Andrea Angle, Michael Ankton, Wayne Baumgardner, Zach Bledsoe, Tracy Drake, Vanilla

Hannah, BJ Johns, Jimmy King, Jenna Scott, Llewellyn Terry and Stuart Thomas.

County Judge Troy Tucker called the meeting to order. Justice Ankton gave the invocation.

APPROVAL OF THE AGENDA: Justice Bledsoe made the motion to adopt the agenda. Justice

Baumgardner seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote

11-0.

DISPOSITION OF THE MINUTES: Justice King made the motion to adopt the minutes by adding his

attendance for May’s meeting to them. Justice Scott seconded the motion. With no further

discussion, the motion was carried by voice vote 11-0.

COMMITTEE AND OTHER REPORTS:

Personnel Committee did not meet.

Budget Committee met to discuss the Appropriation Ordinances on tonight’s agenda.

UNFINISHED BUSINESS: Justice Scott reminded Judge Tucker that the Personnel Committee still

needs to meet to adopt a new Policy to include Al. Tucker asked Pruitt to get with his office to set

that meeting up.

NEW BUSINESS:

1. Justice Hannah made a motion to read by title only A Proposed Appropriation Ordinance 2026-
06 “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO 2026-05 APPROPRIATING FUNDS; MAKING
SPECIFIC ADDITIONS/DELETIONS TO THE CLARK COUNTY BUDGET FOR 2026.”

Sponsor: Justice Hannah.

Justice Scott seconded the motion. With no further discussion, the motion carried by voice vote
11-0.

Pruitt read the Ordinance by title only.

Justice Scott made a motion to adopt the Ordinance. Justice Angle seconded the motion.
Motion carried by voice vote; Yea: Angle, Ankton, Baumgardner, Bledsoe, Drake, Hannah, Johns,
King, Scott, Terry and Thomas.

2. Justice Baumgardner made a motion to adopt the documentation presented by the Clark
County Fair Association for Judge Tucker to review and approve for the Arkansas Department of
Agriculture and Arkansas Legislative Audit for Annual Reporting Requirements. Justice Thomas
seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice vote 11-0.

3. Justice Drake made a motion to adopt the new Gurdon Library Board member, Mary Katherine
Leamons. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. Motion carried 11-0.

4. Justice Angle made a motion to allow Stan Elrod with the Clark County Museum more than 5
minutes to give his report. Justice Baumgardner seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice
vote 11-0.

Mr. Elrod gave his presentation and asked that the Court consider signing a contract of services
for the next 12 months by continuing to give $500 a month.

Justice Johns asked that this be moved to Budget Committee to check funds before deciding.
Judge Tucker asked that the Clerk contact Mr. Elrod with the date and time of said meeting.

JUDGES REPORT:

e We have received a $20k grant to enhance and add additional cameras to the court
complex.

e We had a $209k grant for Courthouse windows. As bids came in way higher, Judge went
to the Historical Association in Little Rock and was granted $100k more. As this is still
not enough for the entire building it will be enough for around 80 windows. Judge
announced that the rest of them will need to be reapplied for with the next judge.

e The 911 center and the jail both have significant roof leaks. We are going to try to partner
with Siplast again for a discounted price on a new roof.

eo Wednesday at 10am we will hold an event planting a tree for fallen soldier, Private Giles
killed in the line of duty in 2005. Immediately following that we will travel to the
Arkadelphia Central Park to plant a Liberty Tree for America’s 250" birthday.

e There will be a Welcome Reception on June 15" from 2-3 pm at the Clark County
Extension Office to welcome their new FCS agent, Jequetta Berry.

eo Pray for Treasurer Karen Arnold as she recovers from her stroke.

With there being no further business, Justice Johns made a motion to adjourn. Justice Baumgardner
seconded the motion. Motion carried by voice 11-0.
