# Where County Commons sits in the field

A list of civic-democracy organizations by function, and what this platform does and does not do. The companion public page is `/kindred` (the organizations themselves, linked by method not by cause).

## The field, by function

| Function | Organizations | What this platform does |
|---|---|---|
| County money and budget transparency | (this platform) | Every dollar is cited to a source page and cross-footed before publication; vendors traced, audits read, gaps named. |
| Legislation and records | [Open States](https://openstates.org), [GovTrack](https://www.govtrack.us), [MuckRock](https://www.muckrock.com), [RCFP](https://www.rcfp.org) | Links to them and offers a records-request kit; does not track bills. |
| Opinion-gathering and consensus | [Polis](https://pol.is) | Yes/no polling and resident-proposed questions. No consensus-mapping or free-text weighing. |
| Participatory budgeting and participation software | [Decidim](https://decidim.org), [CONSUL](https://consuldemocracy.org) | Does not offer. |
| Deliberative minipublics / sortition | [Healthy Democracy](https://healthydemocracy.org), [DemocracyNext](https://www.demnext.org), [Stanford Deliberative Democracy Lab](https://deliberation.stanford.edu) | Does not offer. |
| Elections and candidates | [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org) | Excluded by charter. |
| Method education and how-to | NCSL, Ballotpedia, state offices | Links to them on `/kindred` and offers its own records kit. |

## Platform properties

- **Provenance on every number** — clickable to the source page and file hash. An uncited number fails the build.
- **Tamper-evidence** — a hash-chained, publicly anchored activity log.
- **Channels** — web, SMS, paper and kiosk, through one vote gate with no channel side doors. Web is available now; SMS, paper and kiosk are planned (milestones M3–M4).
- **Aggregate-only rendering** — public pages show counts, not individual votes; costs are published nightly. Identity and votes are kept in two separate databases that meet only in memory at tally time.
- **One process, config-driven** — 75 counties are served from one server.

## What this platform is not

- **Not an elections/candidate site.** Charter bright line. Ballotpedia exists.
- **Not a bill-tracker.** Open States and GovTrack do it; this platform links them.
- **Not a binding decision system.** It is a signal and a record; the republic decides.
- **Not an advocacy platform.** It computes and cites. Advocacy lives only in petitions, attributed to the residents who write them. See [petitions](/petitions).
