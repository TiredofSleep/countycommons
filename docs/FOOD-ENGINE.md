# The food flywheel — how local food systems are financed

**What published evidence says about how local food hubs, institutional buyers and nutrition-prescription payers are financed.**

## Food hubs

In 2020, **91% of food hubs reported breaking even or better**, up from about two-thirds in 2017 and 2019 ([MSU 2021 National Food Hub Survey](https://www.canr.msu.edu/resources/2021-national-food-hub-survey)). The best-run quarter turned a ~4% profit, and hubs above $1.5M in sales averaged a positive margin ([NSAC summary of the survey](https://sustainableagriculture.net/blog/food-hub-profitability-study/)).

The newest data shows hubs are more grant-reliant even as sales grow: **over 50% rely on grant funding** to enable institutional purchasing, and they cite uncertain federal funding as a top worry ([MSU 2025 National Food Hub Survey](https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/increased-local-food-sales-to-schools-and-the-value-of-grant-investment-findings-from-the-2025-national-food-hub-survey)).

## Institutional buyers

US institutional food service spends **over $130 billion a year** ([Health Care Without Harm, Anchors in Action](https://us.noharm.org/healthy-food/leveraging-power-procurement)). Health Care Without Harm, an advocacy group, reports its Healthy Food in Health Care network includes 2,000 hospitals, 7,800 schools and 100 colleges.

Schools bought **$789 million** in local food in 2013–14, a **105% jump** in two years ([USDA](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2016/03/15/farm-school-programs-invest-nearly-800-million-local-economies)); farm-to-school districts averaged **~$114,000/year** on local food, about **20% of their purchases** ([USDA 2019 Farm to School Census](https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/resource-files/2019-Farm-to-School-Census-Summary.pdf)).

## Food as medicine

KFF lists Arkansas among the states with recent Section 1115 Medicaid approvals for health-related social needs; some states' waivers cover **nutrition prescriptions and grocery provisions** (up to 3 meals/day for up to 6 months) ([KFF](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/section-1115-medicaid-waiver-watch-a-closer-look-at-recent-approvals-to-address-health-related-social-needs-hrsn/)). The number of "Food is Medicine" waivers has more than doubled since 2021 ([Health Affairs Scholar](https://academic.oup.com/healthaffairsscholar/article/2/2/qxae013/7595880)).

A peer-reviewed evaluation of **9 produce-prescription programs, 22 sites, 3,881 people** found participants ate about a serving more of fruits and vegetables a day, with lower blood pressure, BMI, and blood sugar, and less food insecurity ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37641928/); [Tufts summary](https://now.tufts.edu/2023/08/29/produce-prescription-programs-yield-positive-health-benefits-participants)). The Rockefeller Foundation [projects](https://www.rockefellerfoundation.org/news/new-rockefeller-foundation-research-finds-food-is-medicine-could-unlock-45-billion-for-states/) $45B in state economic activity if it scaled nationally; that is a model, not a measured result.

## Compost

Composting operations earn tipping fees plus compost sales, and finished compost can lower growers' fertilizer and soil costs ([US EPA](https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food/approaches-composting)).

## Co-ops and land trusts

A review for Co-operatives UK found worker co-operatives survive at least as well as conventional firms; in France death rates were identical over two decades. This is worker co-ops, not grower or food co-ops ([Pérotin, for Co-operatives UK](http://efesonline.org/LIBRARY/2016/worker_co-op_report.pdf)). The first US community land trust, New Communities Inc., held thousands of acres out of the speculative market; its history also includes a 1985 foreclosure after discriminatory lending ([CLT case study](https://www.cltweb.org/resources/clt-case-studies/new-communities/)).

## Shared kitchens

New Jersey's Hudson Kitchen reports its member founders generated **$3.2 million in revenue and 120 jobs in 2024**, and that it has supported 60+ businesses since opening (self-reported; [Hudson Kitchen](https://www.thehudsonkitchen.com/ten-years)).
